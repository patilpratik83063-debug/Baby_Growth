    FAQs

    What does GeoGrowth actually do?

    It researches your market, plans content, writes articles in your brand voice, publishes them to your site, builds contextual backlinks and tracks how often AI assistants mention you.

    How is this different from hiring an SEO agency?

    An agency bills for hours. GeoGrowth runs continuously, publishes on a schedule and shows every change in one dashboard, at a fraction of the cost.

    Will the content sound like AI?

    Each site gets a brand profile (tone, products, audience and examples), and every article is checked against it before publishing.

    Which platforms can you publish to?

    WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Ghost, Framer and custom sites through a webhook or API.

    How do you measure AI search visibility?

    We run a fixed set of prompts for your category across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity and record whether and where your brand appears.

    What kind of articles do you produce?

    How-to guides, listicles, comparisons, glossaries, research pages and business pages, chosen from your keyword plan.
